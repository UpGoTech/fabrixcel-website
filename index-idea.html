Who We Are

India's Premier Structural Steel Partner

FabriXcel is a leading integrated structural steel company providing comprehensive engineering, fabrication, and erection services for India's most ambitious infrastructure projects. With over two decades of industry expertise and a portfolio spanning high-rises, airports, metros, and industrial complexes, we deliver precision at every stage of the steel lifecycle.

Our vertically integrated model — anchored by cutting-edge BIM technology, advanced CNC fabrication machinery, and a highly skilled erection workforce — ensures seamless project delivery from concept to commissioning.

  • End-to-end project ownership — design through handover
  • Digital-first operations with real-time QR tracking
  • ISO 9001:2015, 14001:2015 & 45001:2018 certified
  • 100,000+ MT annual fabrication capacity

Why Choose Us

The FabriXcel Advantage

What makes us different isn't just what we build — it's how we build it. Our integrated systems, certified processes, and digital-first approach set the benchmark in structural steel.

End-to-End Solutions

Single-vendor accountability from structural design through fabrication, logistics, and on-site erection — eliminating interface risk and reducing overall delivery time by up to 30%.

Digital QR Tracking

Every fabricated steel piece is tagged with a unique QR code, enabling real-time tracking from the production floor to site installation — full transparency, zero guesswork.

ISO Certified Quality

Rigorous multi-stage quality control governed by ISO 9001:2015, supplemented by third-party inspection, dimensional verification, and full material traceability records.

Global Reach
